# Backpack

Photograph the pile, get the week.

Backpack reads photos of the papers that come home from school with Gemma 4,
an open-weight model running on your own laptop through Ollama. You get the
dates (with a calendar file), the to-dos, and the papers you can throw away.
Nothing leaves your computer.
